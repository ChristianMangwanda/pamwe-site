Help with the couples devotional app

Pamwe is a private devotional space for two: read the Bible together, reflect separately, and open what you each wrote only after you have both written.

Get in touch

For any question, bug, or account issue, email christianmangwanda@gmail.com. We read everything and usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

Why can't I see my partner's reflection?
Reflections stay sealed until you have both submitted for the same day. Write and share yours, and the two open together.

My partner's invite code doesn't work.
Codes are 6 characters and skip easily confused ones (no O or 0, no I or 1). If a code has expired, the invite screen shows a fresh one; ask your partner to reopen it and read you the new code.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: You → Delete account. It permanently removes your reflections, recordings, prayers, and account details. Your partner keeps their own.

Which Bible translations are included?
Six public-domain translations, including the World English Bible and the Berean Standard Bible.
